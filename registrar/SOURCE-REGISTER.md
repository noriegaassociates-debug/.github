# NMG SOURCE REGISTER

This index registers authoritative and historical source objects separately from patents, repositories, licenses, service-access records, models, measurements, and physical-event records.

## Governing rule

`SOURCE RECORD != FUNDING AUTHORITY != CONTRACT != ENDORSEMENT != PHYSICAL VALIDATION`

A source may establish what an originating institution published. It does not automatically establish downstream eligibility, availability, causation, operational authority, or NMG adoption.

### EPA's Budget and Spending

Source record: `registrar/sources/EPA-BUDGET-SPENDING.yaml`

The official EPA page publishes enacted-budget and workforce history from FY 1970 through FY 2026 and links that history to EPA financial/performance reports, historical budget documents, appropriated-funds controls, the Federal Program Inventory, and USAspending.gov.

The registrar treats this as an agency-level resource baseline. It does not infer that any portion of EPA's aggregate budget is available to NMG, tied to a particular solicitation, or authorized for a specific project. Program-, account-, grant-, and contract-level claims require their own source records.
