# NMG PATENT REGISTRAR

## Governing rule

`PATENT RECORD != NMG AUTHORSHIP != CURRENT LEGAL ENFORCEABILITY != PHYSICAL VALIDATION != ENDORSEMENT`

Patent records preserve technical lineage, claim boundaries, cited prior art, assignee/inventor provenance, and later NMG analytical wiring without collapsing those authorities.

### CN103214250B

Source record: `registrar/patents/CN103214250B.yaml`

The supplied patent record describes recovery of post-service alumina refractory brick by impurity removal, crushing, pitch-assisted balling, electric-arc remelting, melt-to-sphere formation, product sorting, composition rebalance, second melting, fused casting, and extended insulation annealing.

This record is technically distinct from CN106083000A. CN106083000A follows a powder-reclamation and re-sintering pathway; CN103214250B follows a remelt / sphere-sort / reblend / fused-cast pathway and creates two disclosed recovery products: alumina abrasive spheres and fused-cast alumina refractory brick.
